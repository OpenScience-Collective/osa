ON002718 NEMAR copy of ds002718

Face processing EEG dataset for EEGLAB

This dataset is the EEG and structural MRI portion of a multi-subject, multi-modal face processing study (originally ds000117), repackaged in EEGLAB format with augmented event logs and HED annotation. Eighteen participants viewed famous, unfamiliar, and scrambled faces while making symmetry judgments.

How it works
VERSION v1.1.0

Introduction: This dataset consists of the MEEG (sMRI+MEG+EEG) portion of the multi-subject, multi-modal face processing dataset (ds000117). This dataset was originally acquired and shared by Daniel Wakeman and Richard Henson.

Overview of the experiment: Eighteen participants completed two recording sessions spaced three months apart, one session recorded fMRI and the other simultaneously recorded MEG and EEG data.
